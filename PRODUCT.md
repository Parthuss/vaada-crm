# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Owner-led Indian SMEs and their small sales teams. They juggle inbound leads, calls, WhatsApp conversations, and promised follow-ups, often while moving quickly between customer work and sales work. The interface should help a salesperson decide what needs attention now without requiring formal CRM training.

## Product Purpose

Vaada is an AI-assisted lead CRM that helps a small sales team keep every customer promise. It provides one reliable place to capture leads, move them through a status pipeline, schedule and complete follow-ups, see overdue work, and use Gemini for explainable lead insights, editable message drafts, and a daily sales brief.

Success means a reviewer can sign in, create and update a lead, schedule and complete a follow-up, understand the dashboard, and use all three AI workflows without encountering a dead end. AI assists decisions and writing; it never silently changes customer data or sends a message.

## Brand Personality

Practical, trustworthy, quietly intelligent. The voice is direct and calm, with familiar sales language and specific next actions. It should feel locally aware without using decorative cultural motifs or caricature.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS dashboards built from four oversized KPI cards followed by an interchangeable line chart.
- Glass panels, decorative gradients, glowing AI effects, chatbot-first layouts, and excessive rounded containers.
- Enterprise CRM density that requires training before a user can find today's follow-ups.
- “AI magic” language that hides inputs, limitations, or user control.

## Design Principles

1. Start with today's promises: due and overdue follow-ups lead the experience.
2. Make the next action obvious: every lead view should answer what happened, what is next, and when.
3. Keep AI inspectable and editable: show concise reasoning, structured fields, and drafts the user can change.
4. Prefer operational signal over decorative analytics: every dashboard element should support a decision.
5. Earn trust through familiar controls, clear states, and restrained visual personality.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 Level AA. Support complete keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic landmarks and form labels, sufficient contrast, non-color status cues, reduced motion, responsive layouts, and touch targets suitable for mobile use. Desktop is the primary sales-workflow surface; mobile remains fully usable for reviewing leads and completing follow-ups.
