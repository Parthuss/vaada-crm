# Vaada design research

Date: 5 August 2026

## Research question

How can a small-business CRM feel immediately familiar and operationally useful without becoming another generic KPI-card dashboard?

## Sources reviewed

### Mobbin: production interaction patterns

- [Mobbin web flows](https://mobbin.com/explore/web/flows) surfaces real product patterns from tools such as Jobber and Front.
- Jobber's dashboard examples use a stable sidebar, a clear primary workspace, contextual actions near the data, and progressively disclosed detail.
- Useful carry-forward: task-first navigation, direct action labels, predictable hierarchy, and responsive structural changes.
- Do not copy: promotional banners competing with operational work or dashboard panels that exist only to fill space.

### Dribbble: CRM topology exploration

- [Pipeline CRM Dashboard](https://dribbble.com/shots/26443339-Pipeline-CRM-Dashboard) emphasizes explicit status visualization, an activity timeline, and a restrained palette.
- [Sales CRM Pipeline Dashboard](https://dribbble.com/shots/26093707-Sales-CRM-Pipeline-Dashboard) demonstrates a scannable Kanban pipeline with filtering, sorting, deal context, and concise stage summaries.
- [Lead Management System](https://dribbble.com/shots/27181608-Lead-Management-System-CRM-Dashboard-Design) centers lead previews and conversation activity in one workspace.
- Useful carry-forward: compact status signals, dense-but-readable rows, urgency near follow-ups, and stage-level summaries.
- Do not copy: repeated oversized metric cards, ornamental charts, placeholder enterprise revenue values, or a canvas composed entirely of rounded cards.

### Awwwards: distinctive hierarchy and data rhythm

- [Aqtos — The BOSS features overview](https://www.awwwards.com/inspiration/features-overview-aqtos-the-boss) treats operational health as one coherent visual signal rather than a pile of unrelated widgets.
- Useful carry-forward: a strong central condition/attention view, confident typography, restrained accent color, and clear desktop/mobile adaptations.
- Do not copy: marketing-site choreography, hero animation, or novelty navigation inside an authenticated task surface.

## Direction derived from research

- Product name: **Vaada** — the product helps a team keep every follow-up promise.
- Visual strategy: restrained, light-first, high-contrast neutral workspace with one distinctive deep-evergreen primary (chosen over the ink-blue direction explored early on, to avoid reading as default SaaS blue) and a small marigold urgency role reserved for due work.
- Topology: compact sidebar; attention-led dashboard; lead table and optional pipeline view; persistent contextual detail rather than modal-heavy CRUD.
- Signature motif: a compact brand monogram and a continuous follow-up timeline connecting lead activity to the next commitment.
- AI treatment: an embedded assistant panel with visible input context, structured reasoning, editable output, and explicit fallback states—not a floating chatbot.

## Physical scene

A sales owner reviews the day at a bright office desk between customer calls, with limited time and several promised callbacks; the interface must remain legible in daylight, calm under pressure, and immediately actionable.
